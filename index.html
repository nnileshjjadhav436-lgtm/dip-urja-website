About Dip Urja Power Solutions

Welcome to Dip Urja Power Solutions, your trusted partner for clean solar energy and uninterrupted power backup systems. We specialize in designing, installing, and servicing high-efficiency rooftop solar installations, commercial and domestic UPS units, inverters, and battery systems.

Our mission is to help homes, commercial establishments, and industrial units cut electricity costs while securing reliable power. Whether you are transitioning to on-grid solar under government subsidy programs or need an annual maintenance contract (AMC) for your power backup infrastructure, our team delivers high-quality engineering and dependable local service.

Our Core Commitment:

  • ✔ High-grade Tier-1 solar modules and certified inverters
  • ✔ End-to-end site survey, design, net-metering, and installation
  • ✔ Prompt on-site troubleshooting and regular maintenance
